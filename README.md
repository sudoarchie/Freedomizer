## Features:
- **Reverse Scroll**: Scroll down? Nope, you're going up. 
- **Drunk Page Mode**: Your screen wobbles like it’s had one too many.
- **Annoying Popups**: Every 15 seconds, a popup asks: _"Is this really a good use of your time?"_
- **Grayscale Feed**: Watch your colorful feed fade to dull grayscale. Boring, right?
- **Chaotic Keyboard**: Random typos appear as you type—because who needs spelling?
